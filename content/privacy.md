*Last updated: 2026*

Anderson Mill Gun Club ("we," "us") operates this website. We collect only the information you voluntarily provide.

## What We Collect

- Your name and email address when you sign up for our newsletter
- Any information you include when using the contact form

## How We Use It

We use your information only to communicate with you about club news and activities. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, with the exception of the service providers listed below.

## Service Providers

**Buttondown** manages our email newsletter. When you subscribe, your name and email address are stored in Buttondown's systems. You can review Buttondown's privacy policy at [buttondown.com/privacy](https://buttondown.com/privacy).

**Formspree** processes contact form submissions. Messages you send via the contact form are transmitted through Formspree and delivered to our email inbox. You can review Formspree's privacy policy at [formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy).

This site is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. GitHub may collect standard server log information (IP address, browser type, page requests). See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

## Cookies & Tracking

This site does not use tracking cookies or analytics scripts. We do not track your behavior across sites.

## Unsubscribing

You may unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email we send. Unsubscribing removes your information from our active mailing list.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at [andersonmillgunclub@gmail.com](mailto:andersonmillgunclub@gmail.com).
