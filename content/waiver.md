## Before You Participate

Anderson Mill Gun Club events involve the handling and discharge of live firearms. For the safety and legal protection of all involved, every participant must review and sign our liability waiver prior to attending any event.

**This is a one-time requirement.** Once you have signed, you do not need to sign again unless the waiver is updated, in which case we'll let you know via the newsletter.

---

## What the Waiver Covers

By signing, you acknowledge that you:

- Are at least 18 years of age
- Are legally permitted under federal and Texas law to possess and operate a firearm
- Understand and accept the inherent risks of participating in live-fire shooting activities
- Agree to release the Club, its organizers, and volunteers from liability arising from your participation
- Agree to comply with all range safety rules and instructor direction at all times

You can read the [full waiver text](/docs/waiver-draft.md) before signing.
