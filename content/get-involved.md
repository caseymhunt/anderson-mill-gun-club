## What to Expect

Once you're a member, event details come via the newsletter. Events are organized at local ranges in the northwest Austin area. We'll let you know the location, format, and any gear requirements in advance.

First-timers are always welcome at events. Experienced members are happy to help orient you — nobody is expected to already know everything.
