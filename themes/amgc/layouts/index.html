Who We Are

We're your
neighbors.

Anderson Mill Gun Club is a volunteer-run organization for people in the northwest Austin area who care about responsible firearm ownership. We come together to practice, learn, and look out for one another.

No gatekeeping. No agenda. Our only ask is a genuine commitment to safety and mutual respect. Events are shared privately with vetted members — the newsletter is your first step in.

🛡️

Safety First, Always

Every event follows strict range safety protocols. We take this seriously, so everyone goes home safe.

🤝

Everyone's Welcome

First-timer or seasoned shooter — if you're respectful and safety-minded, this is your club too.

🏘️

Community-Run

No paid staff, no corporate backing. Just neighbors helping neighbors get better at something they care about.

🔒

Vetted & Private

Event details go only to members who've gone through our simple vetting process.
